About Stefania

Fantasy author, founder of BiblioBay, and a lifelong lover of stories.

“Scribis, exploras ideas”Write, explore ideas

Stefania Bellini writes fantasy for young readers. Her stories bring together introspection, wonder and adventure, and they are written for children and adults who like to read side by side.

Her background is in Philosophy and the Classics, which gives her work depth of thought and a feeling for historical detail. She is now continuing her studies in Archaeology, and the pull of ancient civilizations and the joy of discovery find their way into her stories.

She founded BiblioBay as an independent publishing house with a simple motto: where adventures begin. It selects and creates stories that encourage empathy, diversity and critical thinking, so that children can grow into thoughtful and caring citizens of the world.

Her two books, Il Carillon Magico and Le luci nel bosco magico, are written in Italian and available on Amazon. Il Carillon Magico is released in English on 27 November as The Magic Music Box, published by Europe Books, and an audiobook is available on Spotify.

Il Carillon Magico

Ages 8 to 99

Elisabetta is twelve years old, shy and reserved. Since losing her mother she has built a wall around herself, until her grandfather gives her a very special music box.

Elisabetta, the young protagonist, discovers it in the middle of the night, just after waking up in bed. Each note reveals a fragment of an enchanted world where music guides the way, and every melody is a message to be deciphered. Her path becomes a journey of discovering herself and an exploration of the value of genuine emotions.

Along the way she meets talking animals, a guiding angel and the wise keeper of a library, and she faces a dramatic moment that tests her courage.

The story speaks of friendship, trust and the strength of kindness. Every meeting in the world of the music box teaches Elisabetta that real magic is born inside us, and that the notes of the heart can light even the darkest moments.

It is a perfect bedtime read, and a book that invites us never to stop listening to the secret music living in every heart.

Le luci nel bosco magico

Ages 8 to 99

In the heart of an enchanted wood, among whispers of wind and sparks of hidden light, a new adventure begins. Every step is a discovery and every choice is an act of courage.

Its hero is Tingo Bao, a little bear with a big heart. He decides to follow a legend his mother told him: an ancient golden treasure hidden deep in the forest. What begins as a simple treasure hunt soon becomes a journey inward.

Along the way, among enchanted trees and surprising encounters, Tingo Bao learns the value of determination, trust and love. The story is brought to life by an original song, “Sotto le luci”, a hymn to the light that shines even in the darkest moments.

A gentle book for bedtime, and an invitation to believe in your dreams even when the world around you does not seem to understand them.

Privacy

How your personal data is used on this website, in plain language.

Who is responsible

Stefania Bellini. You can write to bibobakid@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you send us through the contact form: your name, your email address, your country, the number of copies if you are ordering, and your message.

Why

To reply to you, and to prepare and deliver orders and invoices. The legal basis is your request to be contacted, and the preparation or performance of an order.

Who else sees it

The service that delivers the form messages to our mailbox, the courier that delivers an order (your name and delivery address are shared with it), and PayPal when you pay. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to answer you and to manage an order, together with the records that the law requires for orders.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to limit or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Write to the address above. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Cookies and other websites

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools. To display its fonts, your browser may load them from Google Fonts. Links to YouTube, Amazon and other shops lead to their own websites, which have their own privacy rules.
